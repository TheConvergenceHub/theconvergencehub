Free calculator logic from [The Convergence Hub](https://theconvergencehub.com), a site that publishes sourced tools for everyday money and life decisions. We build tools to give real, usable answers, show the math, cite sources and say plainly what they cannot do.

## What is here

The `calculators/` folder holds the browser scripts that do the math. Each file is plain JavaScript with no dependencies, written for the matching page on the site. They read from form inputs by id, so view the live page for the HTML. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

## Not included

Our other tools (image, PDF, QR and unit tools) and our game OwnYourPath stay on the hosted site, where their extended limits and paid level packs are sold. They are free to use there.

## Want the finished tool on your own website?

You can embed any of our hosted calculators for free with an iframe, which keeps a small "Powered by The Convergence Hub" credit. A one-site license, $29 per year, removes the credit line and works for every tool we publish. The license is issued automatically after purchase. Details and the buy link are in the embed box at the bottom of each tool page.

## Honest limits

These are estimates based on the numbers you enter. They are not tax, legal or investment advice. Rules, rates and programs change, so check the sources linked on each page.
